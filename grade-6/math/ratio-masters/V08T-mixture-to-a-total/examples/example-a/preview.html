You want 15 cups of lemonade in all. How many cups of water do you need?

Recipe: lemon juice + water

The jug is filled with 3 batches. Each batch pours 2 cups of lemon juice from the left and 3 cups of water from the right.

lemon juice

6 cups used

 

15 cups

 

water

Check the answer

Each batch is 2 + 3 = 5 cups. 15 cups is 3 batches (15 ÷ 5 = 3). Each batch has 3 cups of water, so 3 × 3 = 9. Answer: 9 cups of water.
