# Grade 6 Step 1: full standards inventory

This inventory preserves all 94 supplied Forge standards. The completed [Step 2 handoff review](STEP_2_HANDOFF_REVIEW.md) now distinguishes natural and SHOEHORN teaching. [Project map and concept options](README.md). [Coverage audit](COVERAGE_AUDIT.md).

The full Forge description and original identifiers below are source text. Student skills use the supplied learning targets. Depth, within-grade prerequisite links, project homes, recurrence, and individual evidence directions are proposed curriculum judgments, not official DOK classifications or completed assessments. The source’s suggested performance task is retained separately; it does not override the activity rules.

“Core” corresponds to source priority “Essential”; the four source “Supporting” standards retain that designation and remain required in this map. All source rows retain Draft—Head of School Review status. No standard is excluded as optional reinforcement.

Source CSV record numbers are logical CSV records, including the preamble/header, rather than assumed physical line numbers. Every original column, crosswalk relationship, and progression record is preserved in [the structured inventory](standards-inventory.json). Source identifiers link to external standards but their full official wording is not contained in these exports.

A home project starts explicit teaching/evidence responsibility. Where recurrence is listed, later individual evidence is also required. ELA.15, .16, .21, .23 and SS.01–06 in particular are longitudinal responsibilities. An unlisted recurrence does not prohibit later practice.
