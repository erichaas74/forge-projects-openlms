# Template E — Showcase

**Use for:** the final group lesson: the exchange, hearing, fit test or presentation. In math: **W4-G** of every project.

**What students experience:** a performance brief, the live team event with a last-minute change, peer review of another team, and a private decision slip that shows their own thinking.

Shared settings are in the [README](README.md#part-3--settings-used-by-every-template). The live event happens in the classroom or on BigBlueButton; Open LMS holds the brief, the evidence and the grading.

### Running the live event
- Use the observation plan in Teacher notes. The guide's *Prepare and hand off* often gives the timing assumption (for example six teams, short table visits instead of long presentations); confirm class size first.
- Grade item 7 live in **Open Grader** on a tablet; the checklist keeps it quick.
- Workshop phases: open *Submission* when teams finish, switch to *Assessment* for the challenge, then *Closed* at the end of class. Practice the phase switches once in the template course.

## Worked example: M1-W4-G — Run the supply exchange

| # | Item | What goes in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | ① Performance brief | Final fresh offers: A blue 12/$2.64, gold 18/$3.42, ribbon 3 m/$1.65; B blue 10/$1.90, gold 15/$3.00, ribbon 2 m/$1.00; stock 10 each. "Prepare an 18-kit order." |
| 2 | ③ The change | "Supplier A now has only 2 gold packs." Shown by the teacher after teams finish their first order. |
| 3 | ③ Live session | Only for online classes; skip in person. |
| 4 | ③ Final team product | Initial and revised 18-kit ledgers. Rubric: correct constraints, calculations, units, reasoned tradeoffs; multiple valid orders accepted. A feasible all-A order before the update costs $21.48. |
| 5 | ③ Peer challenge | Teams swap ledgers; reviewers check "Is there enough of every item?" and "Are the whole packs right?" |
| 6 | ④ Decision slip | Calculated: unit price and packs needed for one changed item. Essay: justify the revised order. Link 6.RP.A.2, 6.RP.A.3. |
| 7 | ④ Teacher observation | Checklist: can explain one team ledger line; uses units; names a tradeoff. |
| 8 | Solo recheck | 7 items in packs of 4 at $1.20 → 2 packs, $2.40, 1 spare. |
| 9 | ⑤ Project reflection | Reflection, plus "Open items carry into M2 Week 1 retrieval." |

## Before students see it
- [ ] "The change" is hidden and the teacher knows how to show it live.
- [ ] Workshop phases rehearsed; reviewer allocation tested with two test teams.
- [ ] Team rubric (Team product) separate from the decision slip and observation (Individual evidence).
- [ ] Observation checklist opens in Open Grader on the teacher's device.
- [ ] Class size confirmed against the observation plan.
- [ ] Competencies linked; accessibility check run.
