# Grade 6 mathematics scope and sequence

Updated October 2, 2026. Combined Steps 1-5 build using the four supplied projects. Teacher-facing scope, lesson design and evidence specifications; no new projects added.

Steps 1-5 planning workflow completed with a pacing condition: the 48-hour sequence assumes grade-appropriate entry skills and needs a practical trial, especially M2-W2-G. Materials, calibrated examples and final accessibility checks remain Step 6. This is a 16-week project sequence, not a verified full-year mathematics course or a claim of student mastery.

## Course overview

Students move from quantities and operations to signed coordinates and algebra, then geometry and statistics. They explain methods with diagrams, tables, equations and contextual arguments, revise one bounded product per project, and demonstrate their own reasoning in every lesson. Mathematical practices are named with observable actions in the lesson plans.

M1 develops ratio meaning before linked representations, percent and conversion; conceptual fraction/decimal work supports algorithms. M2 develops signed order before coordinates, variables before expression structure, and substitution before equation/inequality decisions. M3 uses coordinate lengths and area before fractional volume and nets; formula explanations revisit algebra. M4 starts with statistical questions and observations, then distribution, measures and displays before contextual claims and a correction hearing.

Each next lesson receives the existing product and specific unresolved evidence, not a fresh unrelated assignment. Required recurrence is mapped below. No general negative-number arithmetic, diagonal-distance formula, formal slope, two-step equation demand or triangular-prism volume is added as a Grade 6 requirement. The work remains mathematically demanding through models, explanations and new applications.
