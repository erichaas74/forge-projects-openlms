# H3 — Ancient Science: Inventions, Sky and Navigation

Updated 2026-10-03. Steps 1–4 scope and sequence drafted and reviewed. Detailed lesson plans (Step 5), student materials (Step 6) and classroom pacing remain to develop. One Tennessee chronology item remains unverified.

[Course map](../GRADE_6_HISTORY_SCOPE_AND_SEQUENCE.md) · [Workbook](<../../6th Grade/Grade_6_History_Curriculum_Standards.xlsx>)

**Inquiry:** How did China and Greece use observations and inventions, and how can we compare their methods?

**Student work:** Study China and Greece in context, compare historical timekeeping evidence, and conduct one small investigation of a measurement principle.

**Final experience:** One paired China–Greece display with map/timeline, historical source panels, comparison table and investigation data; a short demonstration and individual explanation. Shadow data are supplied; the one practical investigation tests a water timer.

**Lasting understandings:** Scientific methods develop within institutions, needs and traditions. A modern model tests a principle without reproducing every ancient instrument. Chronology and corroboration distinguish exchange from coincidental similarity.

**Schedule and access:** Four weeks, eight 90-minute blocks, individual then group weekly. No required homework. Plan captions/diagram descriptions, readable maps, vocabulary previews and equivalent oral/text response routes preserving historical reasoning. Physical investigation access must be arranged for H3.

**Outline status:** These are Step 4 design specifications. Step 5 must select exact texts, verify interpretation-dependent standards roles and expand procedures/prompts, examples and support. The planning clock is 10 minutes retrieval/source reading, 20 explicit context/modeling, 35 project analysis, 15 private evidence and 10 feedback/handoff. This is a budget to test, not observed pacing. H3-W3-G uses the separately stated investigation budget.

### H3-W1-I — Locate China and explain early authority

**Week 1 / Individual / 90 minutes.**

**Learning and project contribution:** Gobi, Himalayas, Pacific, Tibet plateau, Yangtze/Yellow rivers; Xia tradition and Shang evidence; geographic governing constraints; Zhou/Mandate of Heaven and Legalism.

**Resources:** [OpenStax — World History Volume 1, to 1500](https://openstax.org/books/world-history-volume-1/pages/1-introduction). Planning limit: 150–250 words of new adapted prose plus up to two images/maps/source cards. Exact excerpts, provenance and access pending; essential reading stays in class.

**Instruction/model to develop:** Model a geographic governance explanation; distinguish traditional accounts of Xia from excavated Shang evidence.

### H3-W3-I — Plan a fair timekeeping comparison

**Week 3 / Individual / 90 minutes.**

**Learning and project contribution:** Han water clocks/sundials, Athenian court timer, astronomical patterns; elapsed-time versus time-of-day; historical evidence versus modern measurement.

**Resources:** [The Met — Han Dynasty (206 BCE–220 CE)](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/han-dynasty-206-b-c-220-a-d); [American School of Classical Studies at Athens — An Athenian Clepsydra](https://www.ascsa.edu.gr/uploads/media/hesperia/146678.pdf); [Science Museum — Ancient Greek Computing](https://blog.sciencemuseum.org.uk/ancient-greek-computing/). Planning limit: 150–250 words of new adapted prose plus up to two images/maps/source cards. Exact excerpts, provenance and access pending; essential reading stays in class.

**Instruction/model to develop:** Model a source-purpose comparison and one controlled water-timer plan; supplied dated shadow observations illustrate a different method.

**Planned activity:** Select historical evidence from both societies; define elapsed-time question, fixed vessel/hole/start volume and repeated observations; predict limitations; read supplied shadow table.

**Individual evidence:** Individual comparison plan naming sources, measurements/units, controls and evidence limits.

**Quality criteria:** Same comparison questions for both cases; no direct China–Greece transmission claim; modern model is labeled.

**Feedback/support:** If timer is mistaken for navigation proof, identify its documented use and recheck; offer a protocol diagram without filling in reasoning.

**Preparation/handoff:** Prepare adult-prepunched plastic vessels, trays, water, graduated measures/timers; no sharp student construction. Verify full CLOCK access and a Han source record before claiming source comparison ready.

**Builds on:** H3 context, source evaluation and measurement/graph entry check.

### H3-W3-G — Test a modern timer and interpret its limits

**Week 3 / Group / 90 minutes.**

**Learning and project contribution:** Repeated elapsed-time observations; water-level/flow variability; comparison with supplied source-labeled shadow data.

**Resources:** [The Met — Han Dynasty (206 BCE–220 CE)](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/han-dynasty-206-b-c-220-a-d); [American School of Classical Studies at Athens — An Athenian Clepsydra](https://www.ascsa.edu.gr/uploads/media/hesperia/146678.pdf). Planning limit: 150–250 words of new adapted prose plus up to two images/maps/source cards. Exact excerpts, provenance and access pending; essential reading stays in class.

**Instruction/model to develop:** Demonstrate timing a fixed drained volume and honest anomaly recording; changing water level can affect rate. Results describe this model.

**Planned activity:** Assemble teacher-prepared timer; record three short trials under consistent start conditions; compare variation and shadow-table constraints; draft one bounded historical/scientific comparison.

**Individual evidence:** Each student records/reads a trial, explains a data pattern and states one limit on historical inference.

**Quality criteria:** Units and repeat conditions explicit; scientific observations separated from historical claims; no civilization accuracy ranking from a classroom model.

**Feedback/support:** If invented perfect repeats replace measurements, keep anomalies and recheck inference; accessible direct timing/recording participation or labeled supplied data.

**Preparation/handoff:** Budget setup/model 20, project trials/analysis 40, individual comparison 20, cleanup 10 minutes. Confirm kit access and trial durations before detailed production.

**Builds on:** W3-I plan; no assumed completion of science course.

**National roles:** Primary: D3.3.6-8, D2.His.10.6-8, D4.2.6-8; Supporting: D3.1.6-8. NCSS themes: NCSS-T8.
